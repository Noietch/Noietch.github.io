I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beihang University, advised by [Prof. Si Liu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-QtVtNEAAAAJ) and co-advised by [Prof. Linjiang Huang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=j5rBSw0AAAAJ).
I want to work on robotics **full stack** — from building the physical robot ([BRBOTS](/projects/brbots/)), to developing robot-native foundation models that run on it ([EPoG](/projects/epog/), [CR-Solver](/projects/crsolver/)), to evaluating the whole system both in simulation and on real hardware ([EVA-Client](/projects/#evaclient)).
Currently I am a research intern with the <a href="https://longcat.ai" class="brand-longcat">Meituan LongCat Team</a> (Oct 2025 – now), working on robot foundation models; previously I interned at <a href="https://www.bigai.ai/" class="brand-bigai">BIGAI</a> (Mar 2024 – Jan 2025) working on long-horizon task planning over 3D scene graphs.
